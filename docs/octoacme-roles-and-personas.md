# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide implementation quality and technical direction for a project or feature. They help the delivery team make sound architecture decisions while keeping technical risks visible and manageable.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical approaches, designs, and integration points
- Facilitate architecture decisions and document important trade-offs
- Coordinate technical work across developers and dependent teams
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and mitigation plans
- Support code reviews and promote maintainable, observable solutions

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent and supportable technical solution
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Enable developers to make timely, consistent implementation decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews with developers and Product Managers
- Risk and dependency updates with the Project Manager
- Architecture decision records and code review discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Technical Leads partner with Developers on implementation and reviews, translate Product Manager outcomes into feasible technical approaches, and provide the Project Manager with technical estimates, risks, and dependency updates. They do not replace the Product Manager's prioritization authority or the Project Manager's delivery coordination responsibilities.

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads or Test Managers define the validation approach and help the team confirm that work meets acceptance criteria and release-quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Establish test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality gates
- Coordinate integration, end-to-end, regression, and smoke testing where applicable
- Review acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Track defects, test risks, and release readiness
- Promote automation, repeatable validation, and evidence-based quality decisions

### Goals
- Detect issues early and reduce production defects
- Make release readiness transparent
- Ensure validation reflects customer and business-critical workflows

### Typical Communication
- Test planning with Developers and Product Managers
- Quality and defect updates with the Project Manager
- Release readiness reviews and post-deployment verification discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
QA Leads work with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria, with Developers to plan testable implementations and resolve defects, and with Project Managers to report quality risks and readiness against milestones. They provide quality evidence for release decisions but do not independently change product priorities or project scope.

---

## Business Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders or Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational support, and decisions that affect priority, investment, and successful adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business need and desired outcomes
- Provide timely decisions on priority, scope, funding, or significant trade-offs
- Champion the project with affected teams and leadership
- Review milestone progress and success measures
- Help remove organizational blockers beyond the delivery team's authority

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers meaningful business and customer value
- Maintain alignment between delivery outcomes and organizational strategy
- Enable timely decisions and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and stakeholder updates from the Project Manager
- Outcome and roadmap discussions with the Product Manager
- Escalation and decision forums for significant risks or trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Sponsors and Business Stakeholders provide context and decisions to Product Managers, who translate that direction into product outcomes and priorities. They rely on Project Managers for delivery status, risks, and escalation needs, and consult Technical Leads and QA Leads when decisions have material technical or quality implications. They should avoid bypassing the agreed backlog and delivery coordination process with unplanned work.

---

## Delivery Enablement / Team Facilitators

### Role Summary
Delivery Enablement or Team Facilitators help teams adopt effective ways of working, maintain healthy collaboration, and resolve process impediments without taking ownership away from delivery leaders.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives, and improvement workshops when needed
- Help the team maintain clear working agreements and workflow policies
- Surface recurring blockers, handoff issues, and process friction
- Support consistent use of project boards, decision logs, and improvement actions
- Encourage inclusive participation, feedback, and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Improve flow, collaboration, and predictability
- Reduce avoidable process friction and unresolved impediments
- Help teams turn retrospective learning into measurable improvements

### Typical Communication
- Working sessions with Developers, QA Leads, and Technical Leads
- Process and blocker updates with the Project Manager
- Improvement discussions with Product Managers and stakeholders when changes affect scope or cadence

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Team Facilitators support the Project Manager by making delivery risks and process impediments visible, while leaving schedule, scope, and escalation ownership with the Project Manager. They help Developers, Technical Leads, and QA Leads improve collaboration and help Product Managers participate effectively in planning and reviews. They facilitate decisions but do not replace the accountable owner for those decisions.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Projects may combine or omit personas based on size and context, but the accountable responsibilities should remain clear.
